Lesson 1: Historical Chronology

BC/AD dating system, centuries, and time divisions

⏰ Time Units

Decade10 years
Century100 years
Millennium1,000 years

📅 BC and AD

  • BC / BCE — Before Jesus' birth. Numbers count DOWN. Bigger number = older.
  • AD / CE — After Jesus' birth. Numbers count UP. Bigger number = more recent.
  • No Year Zero — Goes directly from 1 BC to AD 1.
  • No label? — Assume it is AD.
  • c. before a date = circa = approximately.
  • BP = Before the Present.

🏛️ Century Rule

  • Centuries start at 01 and end at 00.
  • Formula: take first digits of year + 1 = century number.
  • Example: 1453 → 14+1 = 15th century
  • 7th century = 601–700 AD
  • 20th century = 1901–2000
  • We live in the 21st century, 3rd millennium

📊 Halves and Quarters

  • Half = 50 years. 1st half: years 01–50. 2nd half: years 51–100.
  • Quarter = 25 years. 1st: 01–25, 2nd: 26–50, 3rd: 51–75, 4th: 76–100.
  • Example 20th century quarters: 1901–25, 1926–50, 1951–75, 1976–2000.

⚠️ Tricky Points

  • Oil discovered 1932 = 2nd quarter of 20th century (1926–1950) ✅
  • Al Khalifa rule 1783 = 2nd half of 18th century (1751–1800) ✅
  • Chronology = correct order of events and time in history.

Topic 1: Introduction to Bahrain

Geography, natural resources, population, climate, and history

🗺️ Geography

  • Bahrain is an archipelago (NOT a single island) in the Arabian Gulf.
  • Located east of Saudi Arabia and north of Qatar.
  • More than 84 islands in 2 main groups.
  • Total land area: 786.5 km². Bahrain Island = 80% of land area.
  • Highest point: Jabal Al-Dukhan — 134 meters.
  • Surface is generally flat.

🌡️ Climate

Summer (June–Sept)Very hot & humid. Max 39.7°C in July. Summer Shamal winds.
Winter (Dec–March)Mild 10–20°C. Small irregular rainfall.
Transition 1 (Oct/Nov)Cool air replaces summer conditions.
Transition 2 — Sarrayat (Apr/May)Sudden changes, thunderstorms.

👥 Population (2021)

Total population1,504,365
Bahraini citizens47.8%
Muslims (total population)74%
Muslims (citizens only)99.7%
Expected by 20312 million
Governorates4: Capital (Manama), Muharraq, Northern, Southern

🏛️ 3 Civilizations

  • Dilmun — "Land of Immortality / Great Paradise". Political centre from 2050 BC. 3rd millennium BC marketplace. Trading hub connecting Egypt, Iraq, India, Levant.
  • Tylos — Greek name (4th century BC). General Nearchus explored it. Greek writer Pliny praised its pearls. Influenced by Hellenistic culture.
  • Awal — Name before Islam. In 640 AD Prophet Mohammed invited ruler to adopt Islam → peaceful adoption.

🌿 Natural Resources

  • Freshwater — Underground (Dammam aquifer). Springs dried up in 1980s.
  • Pearls — Dominated Gulf from 3rd century BC to early 20th century. Collapsed by 1950.
  • Oil & Gas — Discovered 1932. Refinery started 1936. Biggest discovery since 1930s announced in 2018.
  • Agriculture & Fishing — Dates most important crop.

Topic 2: Early Trade in Bahrain

Al Khalifa rule, exports, imports, trade revival

⚡ 3 Factors for Commercial Development

  • 1. Geographical Position — Center of Arabian Gulf. Connecting link between India and Mesopotamia. Modern times: goods from Europe and India re-exported to the world.
  • 2. Commercial Ability of the 'Utub — Expert traders. Founded Al-Zubara — important commercial centre after middle of 18th century. Trade relations with Arab coast and India.
  • 3. Political Stability — Gulf had instability in first two decades of 19th century (1801–1820). British imposed agreement of 1820 on Omani coast.

⚠️ Key Facts

  • Indian currencies = most popular. Pearl divers preferred Maria Teresa thalers.
  • Indian merchants called Banyans.
  • Pearl trade collapsed in 1930s due to: wars, price crashes, cheap cultivated pearls, Wall Street crash, riots.
  • Bahrain became main distribution centre for Arabian Peninsula in 1920s–1930s.
  • Goods changed from Indian to British products in first quarter of 20th century.

Topic 3: From Pearls to Oil

Local crafts, pearl collapse, oil economy transition

🎣 Local Economic Activities

  • Most important crop: Dates (ranked 2nd after pearls in exports).
  • Most important income source: Pearling.
  • Pearling was seasonal (summer only).
  • Agriculture problems: most preferred pearling, soil salinity, old farming methods, lack of workers.

📉 From Pearl to Oil Economy

1925Pearl trade started declining
1929Great Depression
Reasons for declineJapanese cultured pearls + Great Depression
Hardest years1932–33 and 1933–34
Municipal house tax inManama and Muharraq only
Customs tax5% on imports normally

🏛️ Government Steps During Crisis

  • Reduced staff + cut government employee pay.
  • Customs raised to 10% on luxury imports.
  • Customs raised to 15% on tobacco and alcoholic drinks.
  • Used Reserve Fund.
  • General public + Ruling Family + Government employees all cooperated without opposition.

📊 Impact on Society

  • Boat building = first sector affected.
  • Divers' salaries collapsed sharply.
  • Boats & divers collapsed by 50% compared to before Great Depression.
  • Pearl value collapsed by more than 70%.
  • Result: unemployment, poverty, security and social disturbances.
  • Oil revenues grew dramatically from 1935–36 (3rd year).

Topic 4: Story of Oil

Evidence of oil, Frank Holmes, BAPCO, infrastructure steps

🛢️ Evidence of Oil

  • Bahrain = first country in region to discover crude oil in 1932.
  • Discovery was NOT sudden — preceded by great efforts.
  • Water well in south Bahrain: Ain Al-Gar (means "spring of bitumen").
  • Bitumen used for waterproofing throughout history.
  • Bitumen-coated jars found in Dilmun era sites.
  • Question was not IF oil existed, but if it was in commercial quantities.

Topic 6: Economic Diversification

ALBA, ASRY, AISCO, BAPCO, Light Industries

🌐 Why Diversification?

  • Bahrain's oil reserves are LIMITED compared to other Gulf States.
  • Onshore field: Jabal Al-Dukhan — production declining.
  • Offshore field: Abu Safah — shared with Saudi Arabia.
  • 1975 decision: set up offshore banking sector.
  • Oil & gas = two thirds of government revenues but only 20-25% of GDP.
